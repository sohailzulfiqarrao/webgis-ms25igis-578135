Lab 02 · 3 hours

District Explorer

Pakistan's 160 districts, built from a shapefile: converted to GeoJSON with measured file sizes, shaded by 2023 census population, with popups, a legend and a province filter.

Plus five extras: a satellite basemap, hover highlight, name search, a province list sorted by district count, and quantile classes.
